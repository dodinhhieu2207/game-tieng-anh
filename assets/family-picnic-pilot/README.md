# Original picnic trial assets

Built-in imagegen,2026-10-29; no Academoo artwork copied. Source PNGs and FFmpeg/libwebp quality88 exports retained. Sizes, alpha ranges and SHA256 in MANIFEST.json.

- Blanket brief: single wide coral/cream gingham picnic blanket, shallow raised view, soft cloth folds, small empty wicker basket at far right and daisy at far left; center blank for live family characters, transparent background, no text/numbers/characters. Used under the Phaser gathering area and the separate DOM picnic game.
- Name-plaque brief: single front-view blank honey wood sign, cream center, teal rim, green leaf ends; soft toy materials, wide3:1 shape, transparent, no printed labels/dotted lines. Used under live word-card text and each recipient name slot.
- Scrapbook brief: quiet nearly flat open cream scrapbook, teal fabric cover edges, central fold, tiny daisy/leaf at outside corners, very large clear pages; no text/photos/placeholder boxes. Used behind live DOM words/portraits and Pixi connecting lines.

Approved six family sprite sheets, word portraits and Higgs clips remain separate unchanged resources. The new gathered sprites use their existing standing/waving frames; this is not a newly generated seated/body rig. Fonts and practice progress use the app's existing shared systems.
