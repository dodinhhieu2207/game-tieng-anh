# Letter Ff production asset prompts

The app uses pre-rendered frame swaps. It does not redraw or morph these assets in CSS.

## Capital F and lowercase f

Create separate transparent six-frame 3x2 sprite sheets for uppercase `F` and lowercase `f`. Match the approved rounded royal-blue letter style: dark-navy outline, cyan highlight, soft dimensional shadow. Sequence: rest, prepare, lift with one sound ring, peak with rings and star, settle with music note, rest with sparkle. Preserve the exact glyph identity and optical centering in all cells.

## Fish and farm vocabulary

Create a transparent 2x1 vocabulary sprite: a friendly purple fish inside a round glass fishbowl on the left; a red-barn farm with farmhouse, green field, white fence, path, trees and hills on the right. Use thick navy outlines and simple polished preschool illustration. No labels or words.

## Real-teacher /f/ mouth sequence

Create a photorealistic 8-frame 4x2 sprite of the same synthetic adult teacher. Sequence: relaxed mouth; lips part; lower lip approaches upper teeth; accurate `/f/`; hold; tiny hold variation; release; relaxed. The upper front teeth lightly contact the inner edge of the lower lip; lips stay unrounded; tongue remains relaxed behind the lower teeth. No text or arrows.
