# Unit 4 Family Park navigation artwork

These seven images are dedicated navigation assets for the Unit 4 card and its six lesson cards. They support the textbook sequence; they do **not** replace the supplied textbook artwork inside vocabulary or story activities.

The mapping was checked against *Family and Friends Starter, 2nd edition*, Unit 4 pages 32–37 in the local source book. The Unit 4 card/banner uses `unit4-family-park-cover.webp`.

## Provenance and guardrails

- Created on 2026-10-27 with the configured image-generation workflow.
- Art direction: warm, child-friendly, soft dimensional storybook clay-paint style; landscape card composition; no written text, logos, stickers, worksheet boxes or watermarks.
- Prompt subjects are logged in `manifest.json`. The art is supportive navigation illustration only, not a source for vocabulary assessment or story recreation.
- All files are original support artwork. The book remains the curriculum source.
