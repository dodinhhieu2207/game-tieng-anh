# Family Show rules

- The game is a classroom role play: a director selects three distinct family guests; a guesser asks “Who’s this?”, says a full “This is my …” sentence, and reveals each guest.
- Keep the three guest order hidden during the guessing phase. Wrong choices receive local, gentle feedback and remain retryable.
- Use the approved family portraits and Higgs TTS clips. Completion stars mean participation in the scene, not an AI pronunciation grade.
- Support tap and pointer drag for setup, and keyboard accessible buttons for the whole flow. Preserve Unit 4 Lesson 2 route and progress key.
