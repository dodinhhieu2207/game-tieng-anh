# Letter Parade

User approved PixiJS + GIF after the proposal. Pixi v8 renders the cart and physical wheel rotation, DOM buttons support the classroom and pointer capture, GIFs deliver the character reaction. Route letter-parade, progress u4letterparade.

Eight rounds: repair K/L/k/l, match K/k or L/l in both directions, then recall two letters with character art hidden until correct. Use approved KeyQuestClips for names and examples. SFX from the existing playful-foley pack.

Local art kit: road from ImageGen exec-ca75129b-b34e-4441-989e-01ca6ace5eb5; cart and lion poses from exec-ccf2362e-7f24-47c9-8ec7-da7d79dbed04; wheel and kangaroo poses from exec-3406e0c1-6614-4563-80ed-19ad34bd7a7f. Two transparent one-shot GIFs (26 frames at 25 fps) share anchors with their resting PNGs. Four repair stencils and four matching piece PNGs were rendered from letter geometry. Current approved K/L boards and Key Quest vocabulary artwork appear in feedback. Letter name and word playback use existing Higgs recordings in KeyQuestClips. Contact and rolling foley use place.wav and roll.wav from assets/playful-foley. Pixi ties wheel rotation and dust to cart travel.

Chrome local: eight rounds at 390 px with real repair drags, wrong retries, both GIF families, visible gates, character hidden before recall, native playing events for all six Higgs clips, and a single completion followed by replay. No game overflow on the first round at 320/390/768/1024/1366. Classroom and physical touch hardware have not been tested. Local preview only.
