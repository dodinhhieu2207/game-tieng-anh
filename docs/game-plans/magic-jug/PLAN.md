## Learning/state flow
Current learning flow: discovery listens to four targets, then six listening questions unlock six capped portions. First two noun questions retain labels, next two hide labels, last two select one J/j among H/I. Prompt must finish or use explicit muted written cue before selection. Wrong choice shows X, repeats same audio and grants no fill. Correct highlights jug silhouette or liquid surface, enables one portion. Held or dragged pouring stops at earned cap. Explicit Next moves on; six successful decisions plus six portions complete once. Small visual starter sip identifies juice and is not counted as earned fill. Keyboard/touch and renderer fallback share same state.
Audio: finite new Higgs V3 Belinda clips letter name, jug, juice, discover instruction, pour instruction, completion. Standalone /dʒ/ not generated/claimed in this first slice; later phonics activity needs separate audition. Real sourced pour foley loop, muted and ducked under voice, no synthesized tones. No background track until licensed suitable source selected.
