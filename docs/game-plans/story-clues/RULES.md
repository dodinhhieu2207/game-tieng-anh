# Story Clues — rules

Read root RULES.md and docs/GAME_WORKFLOW.md. This is a new listening-comprehension game for Unit 4 Lesson 6, authorized with a separate asset set. Do not replace Your Turn or other Lesson 6 games.

- Approved stack: HTML + GSAP, user confirmation on 2026-11-19 after the explicit recommendation. Reuse LearningMotion and local GSAP; do not ask again for this game.
- Keep original book character artwork and teacher-supplied audio. New art is background, controls and feedback only.
- Require playback of the clue before enabling answers. Never use a universally shared greeting as the sole unique clue.
- Shuffle positions so the correct choice is not predictable by location. Use age-appropriate progressive support without a speed penalty.
- Wrong choices allow retry; missing audio, mute and interrupted playback do not count as learner errors.
- Every required asset must be integrated and reviewed before claiming the game is complete. Generated files alone are a preparation checkpoint.
- Preserve App Outfit, existing app routing and progress. No microphone capture or new speech backend is required for this listening game.
- Stop audio and effects on leave, mute or hidden tab; respect reduced motion and keyboard/touch access.
